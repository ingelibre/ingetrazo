# Component sources

- `person_billboard.png` (flat-colour arch-viz cutout) and
  `person_silhouette.png`/`.svg` — derived from "Silhouette of man standing and facing forward.svg"
  (Wikimedia Commons), dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0**
  (no attribution required; noted here for provenance). Recoloured to the
  IngeTrazo slate tone and tight-cropped for the face-me billboard.
- The `*.glb` starter components come from **Sketchfab**, all under
  **CC BY 4.0** (attribution below, as the licence requires), optimized
  with `scripts/glb_to_component.py` (decimation, foliage-card thinning,
  1K textures, auto exposure, real metres, grounded):
  - `molle.glb`, `quinual.glb`, `quishuar.glb`, `arrayan.glb` — "Molle",
    "Quinual", "Quishuar", "Arrayan" by **hanandess**
    (https://sketchfab.com/hanandess)

    (https://sketchfab.com/GiganoGremory)

    (https://sketchfab.com/norgeot)
